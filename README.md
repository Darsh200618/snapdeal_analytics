E-Commerce Product Intelligence & Pricing Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

Project Overview:
This project is an advanced Power BI analytics dashboard built on an e-commerce product dataset. The dashboard transforms raw product, pricing, rating, and review data into actionable business insights using DAX measures, statistical analysis, dynamic segmentation, and interactive visualizations.

The objective is to help business stakeholders understand product performance, customer trust, pricing strategies, and market behavior through data-driven decision-making.

Key Objectives:
 - Analyze product pricing and customer ratings.
 - Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
 - Measure product trustworthiness using review quality metrics.
 - Compare traditional and advanced rating methodologies.
 - Detect pricing patterns across categories and brands.
 - Create dynamic price segmentation based on filter context.
 - Deliver interactive business intelligence through Power BI.
